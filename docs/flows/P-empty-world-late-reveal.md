# Flow P: Empty world, late reveal

**Shape:** a person alone inside one scene with no map → time counted by objects in a corner → events happen in the same world → only at the end the camera pulls out to reveal where they were all along

**Beats** (scene ids `bun run new` scaffolds): `hook, setup, ordeal, loss, alone, landfall, payoff, share` · spoken numbering: **no. The object counter replaces it** · has a turn: **yes (the reveal)** · fits visuals: illustrated-scene, b-roll

## When to use

A story whose force is **not knowing where you are**: someone lost, adrift, stranded,
surviving. The viewer shares the not-knowing because the video withholds the overview.
Example: a fisherman adrift for fourteen months, shown only as a small boat in an empty
sea until the final pull-out reveals the ocean and the drift line.

**Rejected alternative:** showing the map and the route from the start (that works for
a *planned* journey, see flow Q). The map up front kills the feeling of being lost.

## Rules

- **Stay inside the scene** for almost the whole video. One main image (the boat, the
  room, the person), held; information is added onto it.
- **Count time with the objects the person really used** (moons, notches, meals), in a
  corner, filling up shot by shot. Not a clock: the unit is the character's own.
- **Time passing is carried by atmosphere** (day, night, storm, calm) and by small
  changes on the figure, not by cuts to new places.
- **Vary the framing, not the world**: side view, underwater cut, silhouette against a
  moon, top-down. Many shots of one boat stay alive this way.
- **The reveal is one continuous move**: the scene shrinks into a point on the map
  while the map pulls out; the route draws only after the pull-out settles.
- A reconstructed route is labelled "modelled after …" on screen (§5).

## Traps

- Revealing the map early, even as an inset.
- Mixing materials: an illustrated world does not mix with real film footage.
- The reveal framing cutting the destination off at the edge; check the end frame.
