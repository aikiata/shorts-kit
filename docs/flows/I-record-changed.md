# Flow I: The record changed, the world did not

**Shape:** a record everyone knows → it changed value on a DATE → nothing in the world moved, only the definition → close on: what are we measuring?

**Beats** (scene ids `bun run new` scaffolds): `hook, record, change, mechanism, unchanged, payoff, share` · spoken numbering: **no. Dates replace it** · has a turn: **yes** · fits visuals: timeline, archive-plate, illustrated-scene

## When to use

A **named category** (highest, deepest, largest, a planet, a desert, a country) whose
value **changed on a date** while the thing measured did not. Three conditions:

- **Both values and the date have records.**
- **You can name the mechanism**: a treaty, a resolution, a new instrument, a rewritten
  definition. Without it, this is "the old number was wrong": a news item, not a video.
- **The world did not change.** A mountain growing by tectonics is geology (C or D).

**I vs E:** E's two numbers fight in **space** (now, one drawn, one stated; someone is
wrong). I's fight in **time** (each right in its era; nobody is wrong). Test: remove
the date. If the story survives, it is E.

## Rules

1. **The same frame appears twice, not one pixel different, while the number on it
   changes.** That is the whole flow, and it must be *seen*, not said. Reuse the
   identical camera constant for both shots so they cannot drift.
2. **Never call the old version WRONG.** It was right when the category had another
   boundary. "We've been lied to" throws away the point and mocks people (§1).
3. **Dates replace numbering**, on frame in digits: viewers always know if they are
   before or after the change.

## Traps

- **Two changes by the same mechanism make the second redundant.** A video can carry
  two turns only if each change has its own mechanism.
- **Do not end on the new number.** "And now it's 870 m" makes the video a preamble to
  a news item. End on what we are measuring.
- **No timeline for two dates.** A timeline needs ≥ 6 s to read; on-frame text is
  cheaper and clearer for two. Build an axis from three changes up.
- **Records are usually QUANTITIES** (height, depth), which top-down imagery does not
  show. Decide at the brief who carries the quantity onto the screen.
- **Pick an unchanged frame viewers will recognise**, or rule 1 silently fails.
