# Flow L: News explainer

**Shape:** an event with a date and a number → "why?" → the trigger everyone sees → root causes layer by layer, each with measured evidence → what is being done, with a date → close on what the viewer takes away for next time

**Beats** (scene ids `bun run new` scaffolds): `hook, event, trigger, root1, root2, root3, response, payoff, share` · spoken numbering: **no** · has a turn: **no** · fits visuals: news-photo, b-roll, timeline

## When to use

Something that **happened this week**, that many viewers are living or talking about,
whose "why" has a **multi-layer, measurable** answer. 2–3 minutes (still ≤ 180 s),
denser than other flows: viewers come for the news and stay for the explanation. Sign:
the first answer everyone gives ("heavy rain") is right but not enough, and the rest
lives in data some agency measured.

## Rules

1. **The hook is the event, with a date and exactly one number.** "On the evening of
   the 2nd…", not "Recently…". The spoken date makes it news.
2. **Separate the trigger from the root causes, and say where the line is.** One scene
   and one measured piece of evidence per root layer, each with its measuring body.
3. **Density goes on the FRAME, not in the voice.** The voice keeps one number per
   sentence; extra figures go on data cards held ≥ 6 s. Up to three numbers per scene
   on frame, one spoken.
4. **Every claim about the present points to a dated record** (`asOf`).
5. **Publish within 7 days of the event.** Missed: rewrite the hook so it does not
   depend on this week ("the city floods every rainy season").
6. **Default: serious, no strong words.** People are being harmed this week.
7. **The payoff does not blame beyond the data.** Name mechanisms and numbers, not
   culprits. Skip politically dangerous topics.

## Traps

- **Other people's social clips.** A handle is not a licence (§5d). If the operator
  accepts the risk: the handle on frame for the whole clip, only clips by private
  individuals (not news outlets, which carry Content ID), frames that show the
  **mechanism** not the victims, clips ≤ 5 s as illustration between data, never the
  stage. Do not hide someone else's watermark.
- **Repost flags.** A video made mostly of other people's clips is easily kept out of
  recommendation.
- **Listing causes.** Five causes read in a row is flow A in disguise: three root
  layers, each making the next easier to understand.
