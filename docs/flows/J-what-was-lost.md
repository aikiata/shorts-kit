# Flow J: What was lost

**Shape:** the place → what it was → how it was lost → what it left behind, each piece standing alone → close on a new idea

**Beats** (scene ids `bun run new` scaffolds): `hook, whatitwas, lost, left1, left2, left3, payoff, share` · spoken numbering: **no** · has a turn: **yes** · fits visuals: archive-plate, b-roll, illustrated-scene, satellite-map

## When to use

A place, object or state that **has vanished**, where the interest is not that it
vanished but **the traces it left scattered in the present**. Sign: you can tell "it's
gone" in three sentences and still have four interesting things left, and those four
are not linked by cause and effect: they only point back to one root.

Not H (H chains every scene into one argument; J deliberately breaks the chain after
scene three). Not C (C needs a route; J stands in one place and looks four ways).

## Rules

1. **The first three scenes are one block; the middle scenes are separate blocks.** No
   middle scene depends on the previous one. Miss one, understand the next.
2. **The payoff is a NEW idea, not a summary.** After four separate pieces the instinct
   is to gather them; gathered, the last seven seconds add nothing. The right payoff
   turns the present around: what the viewer thinks is normal is the rare state.
3. **Each trace must be something you can HOLD.** An area figure is weak; a living
   species stuck in three rivers is strong. The strongest trace goes second to last.
4. **No "and one more thing" between pieces.** Transition by the object, not by a
   connective.

## Traps

- **The traces usually do not live on the main visual.** Decide how each one reaches
  the screen **at the brief**, and count: in one case only one of four traces had a
  real artefact; the others needed three different solutions (a field of dots for a
  count, a strip of species photos, a before/after pair).
- **Search images by the scientific name**, not the common name. A common-name search
  returned four clean, licensed photos of the wrong species. No right image: keep that
  trace on the main visual, never paste a near miss.
- **J runs long** (four traces × 8–11 s plus three scenes). Accept it consciously.
